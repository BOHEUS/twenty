# Amplemarket

Enrich People and Companies in Twenty with Amplemarket.

## What you get

- **People:** name, email, phone numbers, job title and LinkedIn on the standard fields, plus headline, summary, gender, location, current position start date and description, experience, education, languages and phone details in Amplemarket fields. The person's employer is linked, or created when it doesn't exist yet, and filled with the company data Amplemarket returns alongside the person.
- **Contact details:** choose in the **Contact details** setting whether Amplemarket should reveal a missing email, missing phone numbers, both or neither. Reveals are only requested for people who don't have that detail yet. Numbers Amplemarket flags as wrong are kept out of the person's phones.
- **Companies:** name, domain, LinkedIn and address on the standard fields, plus overview, industry, type, size, employee count, founded year, keywords, B2B or B2C, SIC and NAICS codes, revenue, funding, technologies, headcount breakdowns, traffic rank, LinkedIn followers and all locations.
- Run it from the command menu on selected records or as a workflow step. Each run can fill empty fields only, overwrite them, or return the data without writing anything.

## Matching

- **People** match on email, LinkedIn URL, or a full name with the company domain or name.
- **Companies** match on domain or LinkedIn URL.

## Billing

Billed to your Twenty credits for the Amplemarket credits each enrichment uses: half an email credit per person found, one email credit per email revealed and one phone credit per phone number revealed. Company lookups and records Amplemarket can't find are free. See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).
